# Progressive Decentralization

Decentralization is a controlled transfer of authority, not a launch-day claim.

1. **Foundation-led preparation:** documented roles, test environments, external legal/security review.
2. **Multisig launch:** independent signers, published policies, limited emergency controls.
3. **Timelocked governance:** MIND voting controls bounded parameters and treasury actions.
4. **Expanded community control:** proposal access broadens; operational roles become elected or replaceable.
5. **Minimized administration:** removable or narrowly scoped upgrade and emergency authority.

Each transition requires evidence of technical safety, diverse participation, treasury resilience, legal readiness, incident response, and contributor capacity. Governance should publish milestone assessments and explain delays.
