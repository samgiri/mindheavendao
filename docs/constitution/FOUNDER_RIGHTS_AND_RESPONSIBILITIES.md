# Founder Rights and Responsibilities

## Rights

Qualified founders are entitled to published eligibility rules; consistent treatment; verifiable node, referral, pool, and treasury accounting; notice before prospective rule changes; governance access under approved rules; privacy-respecting data handling; a defined appeal path for off-chain suspension; and claims to finalized, funded allocations before applicable deadlines.

## Responsibilities

Founders must:

- provide accurate information and avoid duplicate or Sybil identities;
- safeguard keys and use approved security practices;
- avoid self-referrals, circular referrals, collusion, manipulation, and mis-selling;
- never describe a node as company equity or rewards as guaranteed/passive income;
- avoid unsupported medical or wellness claims;
- disclose conflicts and comply with governance conduct rules;
- respect privacy, intellectual property, sanctions, tax, and local law; and
- report vulnerabilities responsibly.

## Suspension and appeal

Suspension standards, evidence, decision-makers, notice, appeal, reinstatement, and on-chain effects are open decisions. No off-chain administrator should be able to seize balances or rewrite finalized periods. Emergency restrictions must be narrow, reviewable, and recorded.
