# MIND Protocol Constitution v0.1

**Status:** Draft for community and legal review  
**Scope:** Governance principles for MindHeavenDAO and the MIND Protocol  
**Precedence:** This document expresses intent. Applicable law and audited on-chain rules control where they conflict.

## Preamble

MindHeavenDAO exists to help people develop human intelligence and well-being through responsible technology, shared knowledge, and accountable community ownership. The MIND Protocol is its governance layer; the HEAVEN Protocol is its utility layer; and the MindGlobal Pool recognizes verified community contribution. Nothing in this Constitution promises profit, investment return, medical outcomes, or company equity.

## Vision and mission

Our vision is a global, human-centered intelligence ecosystem in which artificial intelligence, neuroscience, wellness, education, and community coordination serve human agency. Our mission is to establish transparent rules, secure infrastructure, and progressively decentralized governance through which contributors can steward that ecosystem.

12. Technology Must Serve Human Well-Being.

## Protocol layers and tokens

- **MindHeavenDAO** is the principal brand and community.
- **MIND Protocol** is the governance layer.
- **MIND (MindHeaven Governance)** is the fixed-supply governance and Founder Node staking token. Its maximum and total supply is 20,000,000 MIND: 15,000,000 for Founder Node allocation and 5,000,000 for the Foundation and protocol treasury. No post-deployment minting, inflation, transaction tax, blacklist, or hidden balance adjustment is permitted.
- **HEAVEN Protocol** is the utility and reward layer. Founder and referral rewards are paid only in HEAVEN from funded vaults. MIND rewards must never be paid in MIND.
- **MindGlobal Pool** is the contribution-reward pool, not a referral level.

Token ownership does not constitute equity, debt, a guaranteed return, or a claim on a legal entity unless binding law and documentation expressly provide otherwise.

## Founder Node system

Proposed tiers are Pioneer (US$5,000 reference contribution), Guardian (US$10,000), Architect (US$25,000), Visionary (US$50,000), and Genesis (US$100,000), with 365-day and 730-day lock options. Reference values are business planning references only; contracts must not infer a USD conversion method until governance approves one. Exact MIND requirements remain open.

An active Founder Node is a protocol-defined position satisfying the applicable stake, lock, compliance, and status rules. Founder rewards are contingent on published rules and available HEAVEN funding, never guaranteed.

## Founder rights

Founders have the right to transparent rules, equal application of approved rules, verifiable accounting, governance participation subject to eligibility rules, timely notice of future changes, privacy-respecting processes, access to appeals where off-chain moderation is involved, and claims to finalized rewards allocated to them subject to lawful restrictions and claim deadlines.

## Founder responsibilities

Founders must act honestly, protect credentials, avoid manipulation and mis-selling, disclose conflicts, respect privacy and intellectual property, refrain from unsupported medical or financial claims, comply with applicable law, and support secure, civil governance.

## Governance

Version 1 uses one MIND per vote, a seven-day voting period, 10% ordinary quorum, approval by more than 50% of votes cast, and a 48-hour execution delay. Delegation and proposal eligibility require final specifications. Recommended enhanced thresholds—15%/66.67% for major proposals and 25%/75% with at least seven days' delay for constitutional amendments—remain open until ratified. Executable actions must pass through a timelock and published role controls.

## Treasury

Treasury assets are held for protocol purposes under transparent, auditable controls. An indicative operating-surplus policy permits up to 60% for HEAVEN rewards, 25% for treasury and ecosystem growth, and 15% for emergency and contingency reserve. This is governance-controlled guidance, not a permanent entitlement or guarantee. Treasury spending must respect budgets, timelocks, segregation of duties, and available balances.

## Direct referral policy

Only one direct referrer may be recorded. There is no second level, binary, matrix, or multilevel commission. The referrer must hold an active Founder Node; self-referrals, circular relationships, and post-registration changes are prohibited. Qualified direct referrals 1–9 use 7%, 10–19 use 8%, and 20+ use 10%; a higher bracket applies only to later qualified referrals. Rewards are paid only in HEAVEN from a pre-funded vault. The eligible reward base and qualified-referral definition remain open.

## MindGlobal Pool

The initial proposed parameter is 100 basis points (1%) of an approved eligible founder-network reward budget, adjustable by ordinary governance from 0 to 500 BPS for future periods only. Monthly distributions recognize verified contribution through configurable points. Finalized periods are immutable. Distributions cannot exceed the funded HEAVEN allocation. Categories may include active nodes, governance, support, direct referrals, training, educational content, wellness initiatives, events, hubs, country expansion, and approved protocol work.

## Security and emergency powers

Security precedes speed. Least privilege, multisig control, timelocks, independent review, monitoring, funded-liability checks, and public incident reporting are required. Emergency powers must be narrow, documented, time-limited, and incapable of confiscating user balances or rewriting finalized periods. Pausing should stop only affected operations and preserve safe exits where feasible.

## Progressive decentralization

Launch may require accountable Foundation administration. Decentralization must proceed through published stages: documented controls, multisig governance, timelocked community governance, broader execution authority, and eventual minimization or removal of unilateral roles. Advancement depends on security, participation, legal readiness, and operational capacity—not dates alone.

## Health, ethics, privacy, and law

The protocol does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease. Wellness content must be evidence-aware, clearly bounded, and never replace qualified care. AI outputs require transparency and human oversight in consequential settings. Collect only necessary personal data; do not place sensitive health or identifying data on public chains. Legal review must address token, consumer, referral, privacy, sanctions, tax, and cross-border obligations before launch.

## Constitutional amendments

Amendments require a published proposal, rationale, impact and security assessment, public review, on-chain vote, and timelocked execution. The recommended threshold is 25% quorum, 75% approval, and at least seven days' execution delay, but these values require ratification. No amendment may retroactively alter finalized reward periods or lawful vested claims.

## Closing declaration

MindHeavenDAO commits to building technology that serves human well-being, recognizes genuine contribution, and remains accountable to its community. This draft becomes authoritative only through approved ratification and compatible legal and technical implementation.
