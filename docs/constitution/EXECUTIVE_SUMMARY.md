# Executive Summary

MIND Protocol is the governance layer of MindHeavenDAO. MIND is a fixed-supply governance and Founder Node staking token; HEAVEN is the separate utility and reward token. The protocol begins on BNB Smart Chain while keeping EVM-portable interfaces.

The model combines:

- fixed supply of 20,000,000 MIND, with 75% allocated to Founder Nodes and 25% to Foundation/protocol treasury;
- five proposed Founder Node tiers and 365/730-day locks;
- direct-only HEAVEN referrals with active-node eligibility and prospective rate brackets;
- a funded, monthly MindGlobal contribution pool initially parameterized at 1%; and
- MIND voting with timelocked execution and progressive decentralization.

No production contracts should be written until the open decisions register is reviewed. Highest-priority decisions are node stake amounts, payment and pricing methodology, eligible referral base, HEAVEN supply/funding/emissions, withdrawal rules, legal structure, upgradeability, and off-chain contribution verification.

This documentation is neither an offer nor a promise of returns, equity, or health outcomes.
