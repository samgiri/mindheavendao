# Health and Ethics Boundaries

MindHeavenDAO supports wellness education and human agency; it does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, cure, or prevention. Content and AI features must distinguish education from clinical care, disclose uncertainty, use qualified review where appropriate, and direct emergencies to suitable professionals and services.

The protocol must avoid manipulative engagement, exploitative token incentives, unsupported efficacy claims, discriminatory models, and the sale or public-chain exposure of sensitive health data. Consequential AI uses require human oversight, contestability, and clear responsibility. Children's data and vulnerable populations require heightened safeguards or exclusion pending legal and ethical review.

Financial communication must avoid guaranteed returns, equity implications, passive-income claims, artificial urgency, and concealment of risk. Ethics review should accompany material wellness, AI, identity, and incentive features.
