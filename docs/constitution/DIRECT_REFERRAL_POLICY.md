# Direct Referral Policy

The referral system recognizes only a participant's single direct referrer. It has no second-level, binary, matrix, or multilevel commission.

## Rules

- Rewards are paid only in HEAVEN from a pre-funded reward vault.
- The referrer must hold an active Founder Node when qualification occurs under the final rule.
- Self-referrals and circular referral paths are prohibited.
- A registered relationship is immutable.
- Referral 1–9 uses 7%; 10–19 uses 8%; and 20+ uses 10%.
- A newly reached bracket applies only to later qualified referrals, never retroactively.
- A reward must not be recorded unless sufficient funded HEAVEN is reserved.

The eligible base, qualification moment, active-status snapshot, anti-Sybil evidence, cancellations/refunds, rounding, claim deadline, and dispute process remain open. Marketing must not describe rewards as passive income or guaranteed earnings.
