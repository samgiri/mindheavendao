## Preamble

MindHeavenDAO exists to advance human potential through focus, well-being, education, responsible technology, and collaborative governance.

Blockchain, artificial intelligence, tokens, and decentralized systems are infrastructure.

**Humanity is the mission.**

This Constitution defines how authority is entrusted, constrained, exercised, reviewed, and progressively transferred within MindHeavenDAO. Governance is not ownership of the mission. It is a duty to protect the mission, make accountable decisions, and serve the long-term interests of the community and humanity.

## Article I — Constitutional purpose

This Constitution establishes:

- the sources and limits of governance authority;
- the rights and responsibilities of participants;
- the roles of founders, community members, delegates, councils, administrators, and technical advisers;
- proposal, voting, execution, emergency, review, and appeal principles;
- treasury and protocol oversight requirements;
- progressive decentralization stages; and
- amendment and ratification procedures.

It does not authorize fundraising, token issuance, treasury activation, mainnet deployment, financial returns, medical services, or any activity prohibited by applicable law.

## Article II — Authority and hierarchy

Governance authority is exercised under the following order of precedence:

1. applicable law and binding legal obligations;
2. the ratified MindHeavenDAO Foundation Charter;
3. this Constitution once ratified;
4. approved specialist policies and protocol specifications;
5. approved decision records;
6. operational procedures and guidance; and
7. deployed technical rules within their lawful and approved scope.

A lower-level document, contract, vote, administrator, council, or service provider may not override a higher authority.

Where approved policy and deployed code conflict, the conflict must be disclosed immediately. Unsafe execution should be paused within authorized limits while governance determines a lawful remedy. Code must not be misrepresented as legitimate merely because it is executable.

## Article III — Constitutional principles

All governance must uphold:

1. humanity before technology;
2. health before wealth;
3. agency before automation;
4. contribution before speculation;
5. community before centralization;
6. privacy by design;
7. transparency by default;
8. security before speed;
9. evidence before claims;
10. sustainability before extraction;
11. learning before certainty; and
12. decentralization as an accountable process.

No governance decision may convert the humanitarian mission into an instrument of private extraction.

## Article IV — Membership and participation

MindHeavenDAO may recognize different participation categories, including community members, contributors, founders, token holders, delegates, reviewers, advisers, administrators, and elected or appointed council members.

Participation categories must have published eligibility, powers, limitations, duration, removal rules, and conflict requirements.

Holding a token, node, title, credential, or role does not create:

- company equity or ownership of the Foundation;
- a guaranteed financial return;
- immunity from rules or applicable law;
- unrestricted access to personal or confidential data; or
- authority beyond the powers expressly assigned.

Identity and eligibility mechanisms must collect the minimum necessary information and must not place sensitive personal, legal, health, or contribution evidence on a public blockchain.

## Article V — Participant rights

Eligible participants have the right to:

- understand the rules affecting them;
- receive consistent treatment under approved policy;
- inspect material governance decisions and public treasury records;
- know whether a proposal, document, system, or claim is draft, approved, deployed, or suspended;
- participate in governance according to published eligibility rules;
- delegate voting power where delegation is supported;
- receive reasonable notice of prospective rule changes;
- have finalized, lawful, funded entitlements protected from retroactive alteration;
- receive reasons for material off-chain restrictions;
- use a defined review or appeal process where human judgment affects status or eligibility;
- have personal information handled under published privacy controls; and
- report concerns, misconduct, or vulnerabilities without retaliation when acting in good faith.

These rights remain subject to lawful restrictions, security controls, sanctions obligations, evidence preservation, and proportionate protection of other participants.

## Article VI — Participant responsibilities

Participants must:

- act honestly and in good faith;
- protect credentials, signing devices, and delegated authority;
- disclose material conflicts of interest;
- avoid bribery, collusion, manipulation, Sybil abuse, harassment, and misrepresentation;
- avoid describing tokens or Founder Nodes as equity or guaranteed income;
- avoid unsupported health, medical, technical, or financial claims;
- respect privacy, intellectual property, security, and applicable law;
- preserve confidential information entrusted for legitimate purposes;
- follow responsible vulnerability-disclosure procedures; and
- accept accountability for actions performed under their authority.

Governance participation is stewardship, not permission to exploit informational, financial, or technical advantage.

## Article VII — Founders and pre-governance stewardship

Before constitutional governance is operational, founders may exercise documented stewardship necessary to:

- protect the mission and approved brand;
- maintain institutional records;
- appoint bounded administrative and advisory roles;
- authorize research, prototypes, review, and operational preparation;
- preserve assets, access, and security;
- obtain legal, economic, privacy, health, and security review; and
- prepare governance for safe activation.

Founder authority during this phase must be:

- recorded in GitHub or another binding system where legally required;
- limited to the pre-governance mandate;
- subject to conflicts disclosure and applicable law;
- incapable of converting community or Foundation assets to undisclosed private benefit; and
- progressively reduced when activation conditions are met.

Founder approval of a draft does not by itself activate tokens, treasury operations, smart contracts, or public governance.

## Article VIII — Community governance

Community governance should exercise authority only after eligibility, voting, security, treasury, legal, and execution controls are ready.

The confirmed Version 1 ordinary-governance baseline is:

- voting power: one MIND equals one vote at the applicable snapshot;
- voting period: seven days;
- ordinary quorum: 10% of eligible voting supply;
- ordinary approval: more than 50% of votes cast; and
- ordinary execution delay: 48 hours through an approved timelock.

The following remain subject to explicit approval before activation:

- the eligible-supply denominator;
- proposal submission threshold;
- delegation mechanics;
- late-quorum protection;
- cancellation and veto limits;
- inaccessible or treasury-held token treatment;
- major-proposal thresholds;
- constitutional amendment thresholds; and
- emergency interaction with queued proposals.

Snapshots must prevent the same voting power from being counted more than once. Voting interfaces and records must be publicly inspectable without exposing unnecessary personal information.

## Article IX — Proposal classes

Governance proposals should be classified before voting.

### Ordinary proposals

Ordinary proposals concern actions within already approved constitutional and policy bounds, including future operational parameters, routine budgets, grants, and appointments where authority has been delegated.

The Version 1 ordinary baseline in Article VIII applies unless a later ratified rule lawfully changes it.

### Major proposals

Major proposals may include:

- substantial treasury commitments;
- protocol upgrades or migrations;
- creation or removal of powerful roles;
- material economic-policy changes;
- changes to approved token, node, reward, referral, or identity systems;
- long-term strategic partnerships; and
- decisions creating significant legal, privacy, health, or security exposure.

The existing 15% quorum and 66.67% approval figures are recommendations only until explicitly approved.

### Constitutional proposals

Constitutional proposals change the Foundation Charter, this Constitution, protected rights, governance structure, or constitutional safeguards.

The existing recommendation is 25% quorum, 75% approval, and an execution delay of at least seven days. These values are not final until ratified.

A proposal must not be deliberately misclassified to obtain a lower threshold.

## Article X — Proposal lifecycle

A material proposal should include:

1. a clear title and accountable author;
2. the decision requested;
3. rationale and alternatives considered;
4. scope, affected systems, and implementation owner;
5. financial, legal, security, privacy, health, and operational impacts where relevant;
6. conflicts of interest;
7. measurable success and reversal criteria;
8. public discussion and review;
9. the applicable proposal class and voting rule;
10. a recorded vote or approval;
11. timelocked, controlled execution; and
12. outcome and post-implementation reporting.

Material information must not be concealed or introduced after voting begins. Substantive changes require a revised proposal and renewed review.

## Article XI — Delegation and conflicts

Delegation may improve informed participation but must remain revocable and transparent.

Delegates must:

- disclose material financial, professional, family, advisory, and organizational conflicts;
- explain significant votes where practical;
- not sell undisclosed voting commitments;
- protect confidential information;
- avoid voting where a conflict cannot be responsibly managed; and
- remain removable or replaceable under published rules.

Delegation does not transfer token ownership unless a separate lawful transaction occurs.

## Article XII — Councils, committees, and administration

Governance may establish bounded councils or committees for security, treasury, verification, ethics, privacy, grants, disputes, or other defined purposes.

Every body must have:

- a written mandate;
- defined membership and selection method;
- term limits or review dates;
- enumerated powers and exclusions;
- quorum and decision rules;
- conflict and recusal requirements;
- reporting duties;
- removal and replacement procedures; and
- an appeal or oversight path where appropriate.

No council may secretly expand its authority. No externally owned account should remain the sole permanent administrator of a material protocol or treasury function.

Foundation administrators execute approved decisions, maintain records, protect access, and report outcomes. They do not acquire independent constitutional authority through operational control.

## Article XIII — Technology advisory

**SatoshiLab.ai serves as MindHeavenDAO’s technology advisory company.**

Its advisory scope may include:

- system and protocol architecture;
- blockchain and AI guidance;
- security planning and implementation review;
- technical documentation;
- prototype and integration support;
- deployment-readiness assessment; and
- responsible-technology guidance.

SatoshiLab.ai may recommend, build, test, and advise within an approved mandate. It may not replace:

- founder or community governance approval;
- independent smart-contract or security audits;
- jurisdiction-specific legal, tax, or regulatory advice;
- health, clinical, privacy, or ethics review;
- treasury authorization; or
- community oversight.

Material advisory conflicts, commercial arrangements, and access privileges must be disclosed and governed.

## Article XIV — Treasury and assets

Foundation and protocol assets exist only to advance approved purposes.

Treasury controls must include:

- documented custody and ownership;
- segregation of duties;
- threshold multisignature controls where appropriate;
- timelocks for non-emergency material actions;
- approved budgets and spending limits;
- transaction simulation and verification;
- separation of available assets from reserved liabilities;
- regular reconciliation and public reporting;
- signer appointment, rotation, recovery, and removal procedures; and
- independent review proportionate to risk.

The indicative 60% HEAVEN rewards, 25% ecosystem growth, and 15% emergency reserve model remains governance-controlled planning guidance. It does not create a permanent entitlement and cannot authorize unfunded rewards.

No governance vote may lawfully spend assets the relevant body does not control or override binding contractual, fiduciary, tax, sanctions, consumer-protection, or insolvency obligations.

## Article XV — Protocol, token, node, and reward oversight

MIND, HEAVEN, Founder Nodes, staking, referrals, the MindGlobal Pool, liquidity, and reward systems require separate approved specifications.

Governance must not activate or materially change such systems without:

- clear economic and accounting models;
- funded-liability controls;
- legal and tax review for intended jurisdictions;
- threat modeling and technical testing;
- independent audit where assets or authority are at risk;
- accurate public communication;
- emergency and migration procedures; and
- an explicit activation decision.

No vote or document may be described as guaranteeing yield, income, profit, appreciation, liquidity, repayment, equity, or medical benefit.

Finalized lawful and funded allocations must not be retroactively rewritten. Fraud, error, sanctions, or illegality must be handled through published rules and due process rather than arbitrary confiscation.

## Article XVI — Health, AI, privacy, and ethics

MindHeavenDAO does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease.

Wellness systems must distinguish education and general well-being support from regulated clinical care. Claims must be evidence-aware and appropriately reviewed.

Consequential AI uses require:

- disclosure of material limitations;
- meaningful human oversight;
- contestability and correction;
- monitoring for harmful bias or manipulation;
- accountability for decisions; and
- heightened protection for children and vulnerable people.

Personal data must be minimized, purpose-limited, secured, retained only as necessary, and handled under applicable rights. Sensitive health or identity data must not be placed on public chains.

Governance incentives must not reward manipulative engagement, unsupported claims, discrimination, or unnecessary surveillance.

## Article XVII — Security and emergency powers

Security precedes speed.

Emergency authority may be used only for defined and credible threats, such as active exploitation, compromised keys, corrupted governance execution, or material risk to protected assets or people.

Emergency powers must be:

- narrowly scoped to affected functions;
- assigned to multiple accountable actors where feasible;
- incapable of minting MIND or altering protected supply rules;
- incapable of confiscating user assets without lawful authority and due process;
- incapable of rewriting referral parents or finalized reward periods;
- recorded with time, actor, reason, and affected scope;
- subject to an expiry or review deadline; and
- followed by public reporting and a post-incident review.

Safe exits should be preserved where technically and legally feasible. Emergency action does not create authority for ordinary policy decisions.

## Article XVIII — Transparency and records

GitHub is MindHeavenDAO’s official source of record for versioned governance documents, decisions, policies, specifications, code, and review history, except where a binding legal record must be held elsewhere.

Governance must publish, as appropriate:

- proposal text and classification;
- discussion and review records;
- votes and approvals;
- execution transactions;
- treasury reports;
- role and signer changes;
- conflicts of interest;
- audits and material findings;
- emergency actions and postmortems; and
- document and code versions.

Drafts must remain visibly marked. Approved records must not be silently overwritten. Confidentiality may protect personal data, security-sensitive details, privileged advice, negotiations, and lawful obligations, but must not be used to conceal misconduct or material conflicts.

## Article XIX — Disputes, suspension, and appeals

Any process capable of restricting participation, status, access, or eligibility must define:

- permitted grounds;
- evidence standards;
- authorized decision-makers;
- notice requirements;
- urgent temporary measures;
- response opportunity;
- recusal and conflict rules;
- appeal body and deadline;
- remedies and reinstatement; and
- record-retention and privacy controls.

Restrictions must be proportionate. Off-chain administrators may not seize on-chain balances or rewrite finalized records unless a lawful and technically authorized process expressly permits the action.

The detailed suspension and appeal model remains an open decision and must be approved before such powers are operational.

## Article XX — Progressive decentralization

Decentralization is a controlled transfer of authority, not a launch-day claim.

MindHeavenDAO should progress through:

1. **Foundation-led preparation** — documents, prototypes, risk review, and defined founder authority.
2. **Multisig launch administration** — bounded powers, diverse signers, published controls, and external review.
3. **Timelocked governance** — eligible MIND voting controls approved parameters and actions.
4. **Expanded community control** — broader proposal access and replaceable or elected operational roles.
5. **Minimized administration** — removal or narrow limitation of unilateral, upgrade, and emergency authority.

Movement between stages requires published evidence of:

- technical safety;
- meaningful and diverse participation;
- treasury resilience;
- legal and operational readiness;
- privacy and data protection;
- incident-response capability;
- reliable governance execution; and
- contributor competence.

A delay must be explained. A transition must not occur merely to create the appearance of decentralization.

## Article XXI — Constitutional review and amendment

Before community governance activation, amendments to this draft require a documented founder decision, version update, and change record.

After ratification and activation, a constitutional amendment requires:

- a published amendment text and rationale;
- legal, security, economic, privacy, health, and operational assessment where relevant;
- a meaningful public-review period;
- classification as a constitutional proposal;
- the ratified constitutional quorum and approval threshold;
- a timelocked execution period; and
- an updated constitutional version and decision record.

No amendment may:

- conceal a conflict;
- retroactively rewrite finalized lawful records or vested claims;
- authorize confiscation without lawful authority and due process;
- eliminate essential transparency, privacy, security, or appeal protections without equivalent safeguards;
- remove the humanitarian mission; or
- place any founder, token holder, council, adviser, administrator, or automated system above applicable law and the Charter.

## Article XXII — Ratification and activation

This document remains a draft until the founder records approval during the pre-governance phase.

Approval makes it the accepted constitutional design record. It does not automatically activate on-chain governance.

Operational activation requires a separate recorded decision confirming:

- legal and jurisdictional readiness;
- final voting and proposal parameters;
- eligible-supply and delegation rules;
- deployed and audited governance contracts;
- verified timelock and role configuration;
- treasury and emergency controls;
- public interfaces and documentation;
- incident-response readiness; and
- a completed activation rehearsal.

After activation, this Constitution governs within its approved scope and remains subject to the Foundation Charter and applicable law.

## Closing declaration

MindHeavenDAO governance exists to serve the mission, not to become the mission.

Authority must remain visible. Power must remain accountable. Technology must remain in service of people.

We are building for people, with people, and ultimately:

**For humanity.**
