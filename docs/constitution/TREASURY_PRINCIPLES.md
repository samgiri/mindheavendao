# Treasury Principles

Treasury assets exist to support protocol sustainability, security, community benefit, and lawful operations. Controls should include a threshold multisig, timelock for non-emergency actions, role separation, transaction simulation, public reporting, budgets, reconciliation, and independent review.

The current indicative operating-surplus policy is up to 60% for HEAVEN rewards, 25% for ecosystem growth, and 15% for emergency reserve. Governance may change future allocations. It does not create a permanent distribution right and cannot authorize an unfunded reward.

Treasury policy must define accepted assets, custody, diversification, liquidity, counterparty limits, emergency authority, reporting cadence, valuation method, and legal/tax treatment before launch.
