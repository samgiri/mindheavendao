**Approval effect:** None — this audit does not approve or ratify MHC-000

## Scope

This audit compared MHC-000 against:

- MHF-000 Foundation Charter;
- MHF-002 Documentation Standards;
- the existing Governance Framework;
- Progressive Decentralization;
- Founder Rights and Responsibilities;
- the Open Decisions Register;
- the Risk Register; and
- the GitHub source-of-record policy.

The review covered authority hierarchy, status and metadata, founder powers, participant rights, voting, proposal classification, delegation, councils, technology advisory, treasury, token and reward oversight, health and AI ethics, privacy, emergency authority, disputes, progressive decentralization, amendment, activation, continuity, and document traceability.

## Result

**Overall result:** Suitable to remain a founder-review draft after corrections.

No critical contradiction with the Foundation Charter was identified. No language authorizes token issuance, fundraising, treasury activation, mainnet deployment, guaranteed financial outcomes, or medical services.

This result is an internal governance and documentation assessment. It is not independent legal advice, a smart-contract audit, an economic audit, a privacy impact assessment, or a clinical review.

## Remaining approval gates

Before MHC-000 can be proposed for founder approval:

1. founder review of Articles VII, VIII, IX, XVII, XX, XXII, and XXIII;
2. confirmation that the governance model reflects intended founder and community authority;
3. acknowledgement that OD-31 through OD-34 remain open;
4. final editorial and internal-link check; and
5. a recorded founder decision.

Before operational activation:

1. foundation jurisdiction and DAO legal wrapper decisions;
2. final governance mechanics;
3. treasury and signer configuration;
4. deployed and independently audited governance contracts;
5. privacy and security readiness;
6. emergency and incident-response rehearsal; and
7. a separate recorded activation decision.

## Audit conclusion

MHC-000 now provides a coherent and professionally controlled governance foundation. It may proceed to founder review as a draft. It must not be represented as ratified or operational until the remaining approval and activation gates are completed.
