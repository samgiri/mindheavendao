# MHF-000 — MindHeavenDAO Foundation Charter

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft for founder review  
**Classification:** Foundational  
**Technology advisory:** SatoshiLab.ai

## Preamble

Every generation inherits extraordinary technology. Our responsibility is to ensure that technology helps humanity become wiser, healthier, more connected, and more capable than the generation before.

MindHeavenDAO Foundation exists to fulfil that responsibility.

Blockchain, artificial intelligence, governance, tokens, and decentralized technologies are important—but they are infrastructure.

**Humanity is the mission.**

## Article I — Name and character

The initiative is known as **MindHeavenDAO Foundation**, or **MindHeavenDAO** where the context is clear.

MindHeavenDAO is a mission-driven foundation and global community dedicated to advancing human potential through focus, well-being, education, responsible technology, and collaborative governance.

The Foundation is not defined by a token, software product, jurisdiction, founder, or temporary business model. Those instruments may support the mission but may never replace it.

## Article II — Mission

MindHeavenDAO exists to expand human potential by building an accountable ecosystem in which:

- technology supports human agency;
- well-being is treated as essential infrastructure;
- useful knowledge is made understandable and accessible;
- genuine contribution is recognized;
- communities participate in decisions that affect them;
- governance becomes progressively transparent and decentralized; and
- innovation is pursued with security, privacy, and long-term responsibility.

## Article III — Vision

The Foundation’s vision is a global human-intelligence ecosystem connecting artificial intelligence, neuroscience, wellness, education, community knowledge, and responsible decentralized coordination.

Its long-term measure of success is not speculation or technical novelty. Success is whether people and communities become more capable, more informed, healthier, more resilient, and better able to shape their future.

## Article IV — Beneficiary

Who are we building it for?

**For humanity.**

The Foundation may initially serve defined member groups, founders, researchers, builders, educators, wellness professionals, and partner communities. However, its ultimate beneficiary is humanity, without discrimination based on nationality, race, gender, faith, economic status, technical ability, or political identity.

## Article V — Foundational principles

1. **Humanity before technology.** Technology must serve people rather than reduce people to data, users, or sources of value.
2. **Health before wealth.** Financial or operational objectives must not override human well-being.
3. **Agency before automation.** AI should strengthen meaningful human choice and understandable consent.
4. **Contribution before speculation.** Recognition should follow useful, verifiable participation.
5. **Community before centralization.** Authority should move toward capable, accountable community structures.
6. **Privacy by design.** Collect the minimum necessary data and never place sensitive health or identity information on public chains.
7. **Transparency by default.** Material rules, conflicts, decisions, treasury controls, and outcomes should be visible and understandable.
8. **Security before speed.** No deadline or market opportunity justifies avoidable harm.
9. **Evidence before claims.** Health, financial, and technical statements must be appropriately qualified and supportable.
10. **Sustainability before extraction.** Ecosystem design must protect long-term mission capacity.
11. **Learning before certainty.** The Foundation should test, measure, disclose limitations, and improve.
12. **Decentralization as a process.** Authority should be distributed only with the controls and competence needed to exercise it responsibly.

## Article VI — Scope of work

The Foundation may support:

- human-centered artificial intelligence;
- focus, learning, reflection, and personal-growth tools;
- mental, physical, emotional, and conscious well-being initiatives;
- education and public knowledge;
- research and responsible experimentation;
- contribution and reputation systems;
- community programs and global chapters;
- transparent proposals, voting, and reporting;
- privacy-preserving digital identity;
- responsible token and treasury infrastructure where legally and ethically appropriate; and
- partnerships aligned with this Charter.

The Foundation must not present unvalidated technology as medical care, guaranteed financial opportunity, or a substitute for qualified professional advice.

## Article VII — Governance

Governance must evolve through published stages. Early administration may remain with accountable founders and designated operators while processes, security, legal readiness, and community competence develop.

Material decisions should include:

- a clear proposal and rationale;
- impact, risk, and conflict disclosures;
- an appropriate review period;
- defined voter or approver eligibility;
- a recorded decision;
- controlled execution; and
- transparent reporting of results.

No person, token balance, automated system, or service provider is above this Charter or applicable law.

## Article VIII — Stewardship and roles

### Founders

Founders protect the mission, establish initial controls, disclose conflicts, and prepare the Foundation for accountable community stewardship. Founder status is a responsibility, not unrestricted ownership of the mission.

### Community

Community members may contribute knowledge, work, proposals, review, oversight, education, research, and local leadership under published rules.

### Foundation administration

Foundation administrators implement approved decisions, maintain records, safeguard assets and access, and remain answerable to the applicable governance process.

### Technology advisory

**SatoshiLab.ai serves as the technology advisory company to MindHeavenDAO.** Its role includes architecture, blockchain and AI guidance, security planning, implementation support, and responsible-technology review.

This advisory role does not replace independent audits, legal advice, governance approval, or community oversight.

## Article IX — Assets, tokens, and treasury

Foundation assets exist to advance the mission. They must be subject to documented authority, segregation of duties, verifiable accounting, and appropriate multisignature and timelock controls.

MIND, HEAVEN, Founder Nodes, rewards, referrals, and treasury mechanisms are governed by separate approved specifications. Nothing in this Charter:

- constitutes an offer or solicitation;
- promises income, yield, profit, appreciation, liquidity, or repayment;
- creates company equity, debt, or ownership rights;
- authorizes token sale, fundraising, or mainnet deployment; or
- overrides legal, tax, consumer-protection, privacy, sanctions, or securities obligations.

## Article X — Health, AI, and ethics

MindHeavenDAO does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease. Wellness tools and content must be clearly bounded, evidence-aware, and designed to encourage appropriate professional support when needed.

AI systems must disclose material limitations, preserve human oversight in consequential uses, and avoid manipulative or discriminatory design. Sensitive personal and health data require heightened protection.

## Article XI — Documentation and accountability

Official Foundation documents must have an identifier, version, status, owner, approval record, and change history. Drafts must not be represented as ratified policy.

Conflicts among documents are resolved in this order:

1. applicable law and binding legal obligations;
2. ratified Foundation Charter;
3. ratified governance constitution;
4. approved policy and protocol specifications;
5. approved decision records;
6. operational procedures and guidance.

Token, contract, treasury, and jurisdiction-specific documents control within their specialized scope where they do not violate a higher authority.

## Article XII — Amendments

Before community governance is operational, amendments require a documented founder decision and change record. After ratified governance is operational, amendments must follow the constitutional amendment process.

No amendment may:

- conceal a conflict of interest;
- retroactively rewrite finalized records or lawful vested claims;
- authorize confiscation without lawful authority and due process;
- remove essential transparency, privacy, or security protections without equivalent safeguards; or
- convert the humanitarian mission into an instrument of private extraction.

## Closing declaration

MindHeavenDAO is a mission-driven foundation for human potential.

We will use advanced technology without worshipping it. We will build economic systems without allowing economics to become the mission. We will pursue decentralization without abandoning responsibility.

We are building for people, with people, and ultimately:

**For humanity.**
