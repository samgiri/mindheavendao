## Related protocol documents

The Foundation documents sit above, but do not silently amend, the separate protocol specifications:

- the MIND governance constitution;
- the MIND and HEAVEN whitepapers;
- tokenomics and reward-accounting specifications;
- smart-contract and security architecture;
- decision, assumption, risk, and open-decision registers.

Economic, legal, technical, or token rules become authoritative only through their own approved documents and decision records.

## Current status

The public website and Phase 1 Founder System prototype are implemented. Tokens, treasury operations, financial transactions, and mainnet smart contracts are not live. All displayed system data remains prototype data unless explicitly identified otherwise.

## Approval rule

These documents remain drafts until the founder records approval. Material changes after approval must be versioned and entered in the decision log.
