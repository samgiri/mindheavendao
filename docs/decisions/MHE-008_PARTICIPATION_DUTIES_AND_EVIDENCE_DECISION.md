## Approved objective

Founder Node HEAVEN eligibility will be based on documented, useful participation under a task catalog published before each epoch. MIND ownership, acquisition, transfer, holding, or locking alone does not constitute a service and cannot generate participation points.

This decision approves the task, evidence, score, review, appeal, and privacy architecture. It does not activate an epoch, appoint reviewers, approve specific tasks, determine compensation or tax status, authorize token distribution, or constitute legal approval.

## Prospective epoch task catalog

Before an epoch begins, governance must publish a versioned task catalog containing:

1. each eligible task and service category;
2. its objective completion criteria;
3. the evidence required;
4. its point value and maximum repetitions;
5. its submission and review deadlines;
6. the required reviewer standard;
7. applicable quality, originality, conduct, and conflict rules;
8. IP ownership or license terms;
9. privacy classification and retention period;
10. accessibility or reasonable-alternative process; and
11. any jurisdictional, tax, sanctions, professional, or age restrictions.

Tasks and point values cannot be created or increased retroactively for work already performed. A material catalog change applies only to a future epoch unless the original catalog expressly permits a user-protective correction that does not disadvantage participants.

## Approved service categories and caps

| Service category | Examples | Maximum points per epoch |
|---|---|---:|
| Governance stewardship | Reviewed proposal analysis, documented working-group work, governance research, eligible vote participation with required review material | 25 |
| Education and community | Original learning resource, translated approved material, moderated session with deliverable, documented participant support | 40 |
| Verification and research | Evidence-based project review, risk report, data validation, source verification, approved ecosystem assessment | 40 |
| Technical, design and operations | Reviewed code, testing, documentation, security support, product design, accessibility improvement, approved operational deliverable | 40 |
| Human-potential mission work | Approved focus, well-being, responsible-technology, or social-impact program with measurable deliverable | 40 |

The catalog may add a new mission-aligned category only through prospective governance approval and legal/privacy review.

An individual may receive no more than 100 verified points per epoch. Governance participation alone cannot produce a full participation score. Except for an approved accessibility accommodation, qualifying participants must complete at least two distinct approved tasks, and no more than 60 verified points may come from a single category.

## Approved score thresholds

| Verified epoch points | Participation score (S_i) | Status |
|---:|---:|---|
| 0–49 | 0.00 | Inactive or insufficient verified participation |
| 50–79 | 0.50 | Verified partial completion |
| 80–100 | 1.00 | Verified full completion |

Points above 100 are neither counted nor carried forward. Points cannot be purchased, transferred, delegated, traded, borrowed, pooled, or converted into MIND. Unused or rejected submissions create no reward entitlement.

The resulting participation score is used only within the MHE-007 formula and remains subject to the pre-funded pool adjustment and every applicable reward ceiling.

## Evidence standard

Evidence must be attributable, time-bounded, relevant to the published task, and sufficient for an independent reviewer to determine whether the completion criteria were met.

Permitted evidence may include:

- accepted GitHub issues, pull requests, commits, tests, or documentation;
- versioned governance proposals, research notes, or working-group records;
- approved original educational material or translations;
- structured verification reports with cited sources;
- event or support records accompanied by the required substantive deliverable;
- signed attestations from approved systems or reviewers; and
- a cryptographic commitment to approved off-chain evidence.

Attendance, wallet connection, social-media engagement, token holding, referral volume, financial contribution, or an unverified self-declaration alone is insufficient.

Plagiarized, duplicated, fabricated, purchased, automated-spam, Sybil-generated, or undisclosed AI-generated work that violates the task rules receives zero points and may enter the separately approved suspension and appeal process.

## Review and conflict controls

- Deterministic evidence may be evaluated automatically only under published and independently tested rules, with human appeal available.
- Qualitative submissions require at least two independent eligible reviewers.
- A reviewer cannot approve their own submission or a submission where they have a material personal, financial, employment, referral, or family conflict.
- Reviewers must record an outcome, reason code, evidence reference, point award, timestamp, and conflict declaration.
- Review standards and reviewer identities or accountable pseudonymous roles must be auditable without exposing unnecessary personal data.
- Reviewer rewards, if any, require a separate disclosed budget and cannot depend on approving more claims.
- Governance must monitor agreement rates, reversals, processing times, concentration, and suspected bias.

No Founder, Foundation administrator, SatoshiLab.ai adviser, reviewer, or governance delegate may alter a finalized score outside the published correction or appeal procedure.

## Appeal and correction process

Each epoch specification must provide:

1. notice of the preliminary result and reason code;
2. at least 14 calendar days to appeal;
3. submission access to the relevant evidence and criteria, subject to lawful privacy and security limits;
4. review by a person or panel not responsible for the original disputed decision;
5. a reasoned written outcome;
6. correction of calculation or identity errors;
7. a finality date before the reward settlement set is calculated; and
8. an emergency extension process for system failure or broad participant harm.

Filing an appeal cannot reduce an undisputed score unless fraud, duplication, sanctions, or a material calculation error is discovered under a published rule.

## Privacy and data minimization

- Collect only evidence necessary to verify the task and meet legal obligations.
- Sensitive personal information, wellness information, identity documents, and private communications must not be placed on a public blockchain or public GitHub repository.
- Public records should use the minimum necessary wallet, task, score, reason-code, and cryptographic-commitment data.
- Restricted evidence must be encrypted, access-controlled, logged, and deleted under a published retention schedule unless law requires longer retention.
- Participants must receive a privacy notice identifying controller/processor roles, purposes, legal basis, location, transfers, retention, rights, and complaint process.
- A privacy impact assessment and jurisdiction-specific review are required before collecting sensitive or cross-border evidence.

## Accessibility and fair participation

The task catalog must provide reasonable alternatives where a task format would exclude a participant because of disability, language, location, time zone, or lack of access to a synchronous event, provided the alternative measures comparable useful contribution.

Eligibility and scoring must not discriminate unlawfully. Jurisdictional restrictions required by law must be disclosed before participation.

## Work, tax, and intellectual-property boundary

Before a task is offered, its terms must state whether participation is voluntary community contribution, independent contracted work, employment, a contest, a grant, or another legally recognized arrangement. Calling work “DAO participation” does not override employment, wage, tax, benefits, contractor, intellectual-property, consumer, or professional-service law.

Task terms must specify ownership or license rights and cannot silently appropriate participant work. Participants remain responsible for disclosed tax obligations, while the responsible entity must satisfy any withholding, invoicing, reporting, or recordkeeping duties that apply to it.

## Anti-manipulation controls

The implementation must prevent or detect:

- multiple identities or wallets claiming the same work;
- recycling the same deliverable across tasks or epochs;
- collusion between participants and reviewers;
- point splitting or task fragmentation used to bypass category caps;
- referral or payment activity being misrepresented as service;
- unauthorized access to restricted evidence; and
- retroactive rule changes or score tampering.

Material suspected misconduct must follow published notice, evidence, suspension, and appeal rules rather than an undocumented discretionary confiscation process.

## Required publication before activation

A participation epoch cannot activate until the repository records:

- the final task catalog and scoring specification;
- reviewer eligibility, appointment, independence, and removal rules;
- the evidence system and security assessment;
- the privacy impact assessment and retention schedule;
- the appeal panel and operating deadlines;
- the relevant HEAVEN pool and MHE-007 epoch parameters;
- accounting, tax, IP, labor, sanctions, and jurisdictional review;
- smart-contract or calculation-engine tests and independent security review; and
- the final governance and legal activation decision.

## Legal and communications boundary

Participation rewards are variable and may be zero. This framework is intended to connect HEAVEN eligibility to genuine ecosystem services, but it does not itself establish that MIND, HEAVEN, a Founder Node, an allocation epoch, or a reward arrangement is outside securities or other financial regulation.

Public materials must accurately describe the actual work, evidence, review, risks, funding limits, and jurisdictional restrictions. They must not promise passive income, guaranteed profit, fixed yield, employment, tax treatment, or regulatory approval.
