## Approved objective

MindHeavenDAO will use fixed MIND quantities for Founder Node allocations. It will not use an automatic bonding curve, user-count multiplier, purchase-by-purchase price escalation, price oracle, peg, or programmed appreciation mechanism.

Legality, accurate communications, user protection, and sustainable pre-funding are mandatory launch gates. This Founder approval does not constitute legal approval and does not authorize a public offer, sale, contribution flow, contract activation, liquidity program, reward epoch, or deployment.

| Founder Node tier | Fixed MIND quantity |
|---|---:|

These quantities remain governed by MHE-003.

## Epoch reference model

A future governance-approved allocation epoch may establish an off-chain contribution reference rate. The planning formula is:

```text
Required contribution reference =
fixed MIND quantity × approved epoch reference rate
```

The formula is an off-chain planning and allocation tool only. Smart contracts must operate in fixed token quantities and must not read or enforce a USD price.

Each proposed epoch must publish, before it begins:

1. its opening and closing conditions;
2. its fixed MIND allocation capacity;
3. its off-chain contribution reference rate and methodology;
4. its accepted contribution assets, if legally approved;
5. its treasury and development purpose;
6. its jurisdictional eligibility and exclusions;
7. its KYC, AML, sanctions, consumer-protection, tax, and privacy controls;
8. its refund, cancellation, custody, and dispute terms;
9. its pre-funded HEAVEN reward budget, if any; and
10. the legal, financial, security, and governance approvals required for activation.

An approved reference rate must remain unchanged during its epoch. A later rate requires a separately documented prospective decision and cannot modify an active epoch or an existing participant position retroactively.

## Reference-rate boundaries

The epoch reference rate:

- is not MIND's market price, fair value, exchange rate, peg, redemption value, or guaranteed future value;
- does not create an obligation for the Foundation, treasury, DAO, SatoshiLab.ai, or any contributor to purchase or redeem MIND;
- cannot be automatically increased because more users participate;
- cannot be represented as evidence that MIND will appreciate;
- cannot create a minimum return, guaranteed yield, or entitlement to HEAVEN;
- cannot be used by a smart contract as a USD-denominated accounting source; and
- may be changed only prospectively through the approved governance and legal-readiness process.

The Genesis planning reference may remain 1 MIND = USD 1 under MHE-002. Future epoch references are not pre-approved by this decision.

## Reward separation

Founder Node allocation and HEAVEN reward funding are separate controls.

- No allocation epoch automatically activates a reward epoch.
- HEAVEN rewards require a separately approved and pre-funded epoch budget.
- Any actual reward rate must remain below the applicable maximum ceiling in MHE-004.
- Claim options remain governed by MHE-005.
- Actual rewards may be lower than an illustration or zero.
- No reward may be created beyond the available funded pool.
- No document may characterize a maximum ceiling, allocation factor, or planning reference as APY, guaranteed income, investment return, or promised profit.

## Communications standard

Public and participant-facing materials must not use statements such as:

- “buy early before the price increases”;
- “invest in a Founder Node”;
- “guaranteed return,” “fixed income,” or “assured APY”;
- “1 MIND is guaranteed to equal USD 1”; or
- “SEC compliant” or “legally approved” without a written, applicable professional determination.

Preferred language is “participate,” “commit,” “fixed MIND allocation,” “off-chain planning reference,” “variable pre-funded reward,” and “subject to jurisdiction-specific eligibility and legal approval.”

Labels do not override economic substance. All communications must accurately describe how the system actually operates.

## Global legal-readiness gates

Before any allocation or reward epoch may activate, the Foundation must obtain and record:

1. a jurisdiction and legal-wrapper decision;
2. a market-by-market legal analysis covering securities, financial promotion, consumer protection, payments, custody, tax, and data protection;
3. AML, KYC, sanctions, source-of-funds, transaction-monitoring, and reporting controls appropriate to the activities and jurisdictions;
4. approved terms, risk disclosures, privacy notices, and complaint/refund processes;
5. treasury evidence showing the relevant HEAVEN reward pool is pre-funded;
6. independent smart-contract security review and remediation;
7. governance approval of the exact epoch parameters; and
8. a written activation record identifying permitted and excluded markets.

If any required approval is absent, uncertain, expired, or jurisdictionally inapplicable, the epoch remains inactive for the affected market.

## No legal certification

This record is a governance and design decision. It is not a legal opinion, regulatory safe harbor, registration, exemption, or certification of compliance with SEC rules or any other jurisdiction's law.

## Effective scope

This decision approves the fixed-quantity, governance-approved epoch planning model and establishes legal readiness as a mandatory activation gate. It rejects automatic price escalation and speculative appreciation messaging.

It does not approve a future reference rate, accepted payment asset, public contribution flow, legal wrapper, jurisdiction, HEAVEN emission or funding source, reward formula, liquidity program, smart contract, deployment, or launch.
